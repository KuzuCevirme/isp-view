Always visible to your ISP

  • Your public IP address, and so your rough location and your account.
  • Every server IP you connect to, plus the port, protocol, timing and amount of data (see "Connections").
  • Website names from plain DNS lookups, the HTTPS handshake (SNI) and the first QUIC/HTTP3 packet.
  • For unencrypted http:// sites: the full URL, headers and page content.

Not visible with HTTPS

  • The page or path inside a site (for example /watch?v=…), search terms, form data, passwords, messages and cookies.
  • The page content itself.

How to hide more

  • Encrypted DNS (DoH/DoT, in Windows or your browser) hides lookups, but SNI still shows site names.
  • ECH (Encrypted Client Hello, supported by Chrome/Firefox with sites behind Cloudflare and similar) hides the SNI too.
  • A VPN hides everything above, but your ISP still sees the VPN server and how much data you send. Your VPN provider then sees what your ISP saw.
